## Public learner experience (V0.1.4.4 candidate)

Coverage Labs is presented as an open fictional investigative-journalism playground. The landing page offers direct choice: Tutorial, Academy, Exam or browse the fictional web. The Academy is the central workbench. Learner-facing copy does not prescribe a personal journey or expose facilitator-only guidance. All people, entities, records, motives and events are fictional simulation material.


## V0.1.4.4 — Open World & Academy Refresh

Public entry is now choice-based: Tutorial, Academy, Exam or browse the fictional web. The Academy is the central workbench with ten updated modules, an evidence-and-motives method and a competing-explanations ledger. GoedGeregeld is discovered through VINDR advertising and site placements; its own records are available within its site but excluded from ordinary VINDR indexing. Kompas is the independent member-owned counterexample and does not deploy GoedGeregeld. The institutional ecosystem now includes energy, infrastructure, investment, family-office and capital entities with their own sites, wikis and source records.
