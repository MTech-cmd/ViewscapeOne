# Coverage Labs — Project Definition

## Purpose

Coverage Labs is an open, local-first fictional investigative journalism training playground. It teaches learners to identify claims, map organisations, evaluate sources, connect evidence, assess findings and write transparent reports.

## Current milestone

**V0.1.4.3 — Public Playground, deployment-safe navigation, company logos and GoedGeregeld.** The project includes the independent foundation, Solstice guided tutorial, Frequency worked investigation, Palisade independent examination, VINDR local search engine, KennisKade source-cited wiki and expanded fictional external institutions.

## Governance

- Operator controls priorities, acceptance and milestone progression.
- Maintainer coordinates review and alignment.
- Development implements and tests authorised work.
- Research contributes structured evidence and source work when authorised.
- No milestone is self-approved by Development.

## Fictional-world rule

All company groups, providers, institutions, people, claims, metrics, documents and reports in Coverage Labs are fictional training material. They are not real-world Dutch insurance evidence.

## External information boundaries

VINDR searches only the local Coverage Labs application corpus; it does not connect to the public internet. Its sponsored results are fictional advertisements. KennisKade article edits are browser-local and source-ID validated; they are not collaboratively published or synchronised. All institutional records and entities remain fictional.

## Playthrough gate

The project owner must complete the Coverage Labs learning journey and explicitly authorise the next phase before any Dutch Health Insurance Picker research or development begins.
