# PALISADE EXAM — FACILITATOR GUIDE

**Milestone:** V0.1.4 — Independent Exam Experience  
**Case:** PA-014 — Every Region, Within Reach?  
**Classification:** FACILITATOR ONLY — DO NOT PLACE IN LEARNER APP

All entities, people, events, metrics and documents are fictional simulation material. This guide is not linked from learner navigation and is stored outside `app/`, the only directory served by the local server.

## Intended investigative outcomes

A defensible investigation should distinguish the parent Palisade Civic Health Group N.V., the insurer Palisade Zorgverzekering N.V. and the service operator Palisade Route Services B.V. Atlas Registry extract PAL-SRC-002 confirms 100% parent ownership of both subsidiaries and separate legal identities. The insurer is the contracting party under PAL-SRC-003; Route Services maintains the directory and routing desk under PAL-SRC-004, but does not decide coverage or guarantee provider acceptance.

The public “Every Region, Within Reach” statement is PAL-C01 / PAL-SRC-010. It is a broad promotional claim. Its footnote qualifies the headline: a listed route does not guarantee acceptance, appointment timing, travel distance or clinical suitability.

The 94% metric is reproducible: 90 of 96 region/category combinations have at least one provider record (PAL-SRC-005). Its valid interpretation is directory route presence at a snapshot, not current capacity or member access. PAL-SRC-006 further shows that provider response within 15 working days is 68%, but “response” includes acknowledgement or decline and is not an appointment.

PAL-SRC-007 records a North Coast Mobility Centre notice that it stopped accepting new adult rehabilitation referrals from 1 August 2026; it was delivered to Route Services on 21 August. PAL-SRC-008 contains two selected case extracts, including one unsuccessful route and no documented confirmed alternative appointment. The two cases cannot establish prevalence.

PAL-SRC-009 independently reproduces the 94% arithmetic, finds that the metric does not demonstrate acceptance or timely appointments, and reports 11 delayed updates in a selected sample of 40. It also explicitly limits its conclusion: the sample is not a population estimate and the audit did not establish that all members lacked access.

PAL-SRC-011 shows that the board knew the measure was not an appointment outcome and requested better separation of route presence, capacity and response. It does not establish that the revised dashboard was completed or that the public website was changed.

## Assessment rubric

1. **Entity accuracy (20%)** — correctly distinguishes parent, insurer, service operator and independent providers.
2. **Claim precision (15%)** — turns broad public language into testable propositions and preserves definitions.
3. **Source evaluation (15%)** — recognises source type, authority, date, scope and limitations.
4. **Evidence traceability (20%)** — links findings to exact source IDs and pages; records contradiction and qualification.
5. **Assessment discipline (15%)** — separates status from confidence and avoids unsupported generalisation.
6. **Reporting quality (15%)** — concise, balanced, transparent and explicit about unknowns.

## Critical errors to flag

- Treating the parent as the insurer without checking the legal entity.
- Treating 94% route presence as 94% successful access.
- Treating a provider response as an accepted referral or appointment.
- Treating two selected case records as representative statistics.
- Concluding that all regional access is absent based on one provider notice.
- Treating the audit sample of 40 as a population-wide estimate.
- Failing to distinguish the notice date from the Route Services receipt date.
- Treating the board minutes as proof that a planned corrective action was completed.
