# FACILITATOR GUIDE — V0.1.3 FREQUENCY WORKED EXAMPLE

**Classification:** Facilitator-only teaching material. Do not place in learner navigation or served application.
**Case:** FE-013 — One Call. One Answer?
**All entities, records, metrics and reporting are fictional.**

## Intended learning points

1. Separate brand unity from legal identity and operational responsibility.
2. Treat the 88% as a valid calculation of eligible interaction records marked RESOLVED under TUNE v3.2, not as a verified member-issue outcome rate.
3. Distinguish interaction closure from resolution of the underlying member issue.
4. Use a biased convenience sample to identify possible failure modes, not to estimate prevalence.
5. Interpret the 92% network metric as slot visibility in a feed, not booked and suitable appointment access.
6. Avoid inferring deliberate deception from an overbroad public impression and internal awareness alone.
7. Preserve missing member-level outcome data, missing contract schedules, ownership of Frequency Network, and action completion as unknowns.

## Teaching cautions

- Do not call the 88% calculation fabricated or arithmetically false.
- Do not extrapolate the 17/40 sample result to the full member population.
- Do not assume Frequency Network is owned by the parent; the register extract does not establish that.
- Do not infer that a contract clause proves actual compliance.
- Do not infer intent from evidence of awareness of a measurement limitation.
