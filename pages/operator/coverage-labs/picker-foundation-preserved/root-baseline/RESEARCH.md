# Research (Res) — Coordination Contract

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Initial coordination draft; requires Res review

## Purpose

Define the information Development needs to represent research without taking ownership of research conclusions.

## Research responsibilities

Res investigates groups, providers and individual policies; maintains evidence methodology; prepares structured findings; and records uncertainty, limitations and conflicting information.

## Required evidence metadata

Where available, provide source title, publisher, direct URL, publication date, date checked, applicable entity level and identifier, concise evidence summary, limitations, confidence and conflicting information. Distinguish primary evidence, secondary reporting, corporate statements and independent assessments.

## Claim handling

A claim must identify its subject entity, category, precise finding, supporting source identifiers, limitations, confidence and last verification date. Do not create a positive or negative assessment solely because evidence is absent. Corporate commitments must not be presented as demonstrated outcomes without implementation evidence.

## Coordination required before schema freeze

Res should review `schemas/claim.schema.json` and `schemas/source.schema.json`, especially category vocabulary, confidence vocabulary, source types, conflicting-evidence representation, verification dates and whether evidence summaries belong on source records, claim records or both.

## Current research state

No verified 2027 policy data, premiums, provider-network data, corporate assessments or scored findings are included in this V0.1 foundation. The five group/provider pairs are initial research subjects only; legal identities and relationships remain to be verified.
