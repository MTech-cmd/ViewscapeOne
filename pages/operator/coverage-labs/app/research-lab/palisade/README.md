# PALISADE — fictional exam company

Palisade is a fictional training world created for Coverage Labs V0.1.4. Its visual identity draws on late-1970s European airport wayfinding, civic information terminals and printed travel ephemera. The current visual palette is witchy pastel sage green, lavender and purple-goth: deep aubergine, muted violet, soft lilac and pale green. All entities, people, contracts, metrics, records and events are invented.

The exam case is **Every Region, Within Reach?** The learner must independently investigate the public access claim, corporate structure, delegated operations, metric definition, source limitations and unresolved member outcomes.

Learner entry points: `website/index.html`, `wiki/index.html`, `research/desk.html`.
