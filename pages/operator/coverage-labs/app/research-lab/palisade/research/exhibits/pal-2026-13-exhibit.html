Regional service contract renewal and cost-to-serve review

Publication date: 2026-11-14 · Strategy and finance committee extract

The finance committee compares regional service contracts by cost per completed route and renewal probability. The paper recommends consolidating low-volume partner agreements while preserving a minimum regional coverage threshold. It does not establish that a specific patient was denied care or that a route completion equals treatment.

Limitations: this is a management-authored proposal, not evidence of implementation or member-level outcomes.
