# Rivierzorg Direct - Corporate and Product History

> Fictional timeline reconstructed from the scenario source pack.

| Year | Recorded event |
|---|---|
| 2018 | Riviergroep is founded by a technology-led investment consortium to build a lower-cost digital insurer. |
| 2019 | Rivierzorg launches a budget naturapolis supported by an app-first claims engine. |
| 2020 | A preferred-provider network replaces the broad open network used in the pilot. |
| 2022 | The group rolls out FlowCore, an automated authorisation and claims-routing platform. |
| 2025 | The platform is connected to a third-party case and document relay for exceptions and manual review. |
| 2026 | A service interruption delays a subset of document-dependent reimbursements; the group reports recovery within two working days. |
| 2027 | The basic premium remains the lowest in this scenario, alongside the lowest non-contracted reimbursement rate. |

## Identity changes

The simulation distinguishes the founding mutual/group, the contemporary parent group, the operating insurer and the public brand. These layers should never be collapsed into a single `company_name` field.

## Research questions

- Did a change in ownership, legal form or operating entity occur?
- Which subsidiaries provide insurance, administration, navigation, technology or procurement?
