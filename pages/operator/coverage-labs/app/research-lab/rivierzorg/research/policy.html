Fictional source environment: Rivierzorg Direct, all legal identities, people, documents, premiums, provider networks and metrics are invented. No actual coverage or legal position is described.

Product identity and scope

Policy year 2027 · Legal insurer: Rivierzorg Verzekeringen N.V. · Parent group: Riviergroep N.V.

The assumed €385 deductible is a fictional scenario parameter. It is not a statement of the real statutory deductible. The basic policy and supplementary products must be analysed as separate contracts.

Basic insurance

Rivier Basis Select

€145.50 / month

  • Budget naturapolis; annual basic premium €1,746.00.
  • Non-contracted care: 60% of the average contracted tariff subject to service-specific maximum amounts.
  • Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
  • The 78% network headline measures hospital addresses marked active in a directory snapshot, not contracted specialties or available appointments.

Source: RIVIERZORG-SRC-002 and RIVIERZORG-SRC-007

Supplementary

Rivier Start

€15.95 / month

  • 6 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €250 per policy year.
  • Some complex dental treatments have a 12-month waiting period.
  • App-based claims are supported; paper submission remains available for exceptions.

Source: RIVIERZORG-SRC-002

Supplementary

Rivier Stroom

€27.50 / month

  • 9 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €400 per policy year.
  • Selected optical contribution up to €100 every two calendar years.
  • Treatment at non-preferred supplementary providers may receive no benefit unless pre-authorised.

Source: RIVIERZORG-SRC-002

Policy interpretation checks

  1. Confirm the legal insurer and policy version.
  2. Read the reimbursement tariff definition and maximum amounts.
  3. Check prior authorisation, referral and clinical indication rules.
  4. Separate per-year limits from per-treatment limits.
  5. Check waiting periods and planned-treatment exclusions.
  6. Check provider identity, location and policy-year contract status.
