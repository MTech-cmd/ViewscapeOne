# Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg - Fictional Company Wiki

> **FICTIONAL SIMULATION.** This is a secondary training article. The organisation, people, policies, prices, documents, events and figures are invented. It is not a real company, insurer, policy or legal record.

## Overview

A regional cooperative built around family support, member participation and care navigation. Its warm brand hides a precise eligibility system: who counts as family, who is a member, and where the regional promise applies are separate questions.

## Corporate profile

- **Parent group:** Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A.
- **Legal insurer:** Kompas Zorgverzekeraar U.A.
- **Public brand:** Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg
- **Registered office (scenario):** Amersfoort
- **Founded (scenario):** 1978
- **Insured population:** 250,000
- **Scenario market share:** 3.2% (scenario estimate)
- **Company model:** Regional member cooperative

## History

- **1978:** Local member societies in Utrecht and Gelderland establish a cooperative health fund.
- **1994:** The cooperative adds family assistance desks and a travelling care-navigation service.
- **2008:** A national basic policy is introduced while local member assemblies retain advisory powers.
- **2016:** The group creates a formal care-navigation subsidiary and a regional partner directory.
- **2023:** A family-support pilot expands to include a named navigator for eligible households.
- **2025:** Members debate the distinction between insured household lives and voting cooperative members.
- **2027:** Kompas reports the broadest hospital network in this scenario, concentrated in its three core provinces.

## Insurance products

### Kompas Ruime Keuze

**Basic insurance - €168.90 / month**
- Ruime naturapolis; annual basic premium €2,026.80.
- Non-contracted care: 85% of the insurer-defined average contracted tariff, subject to policy terms and statutory limits.
- Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
- The 95% hospital figure counts hospital organisations with any active contract; the source does not establish the same level of access in every province or specialty.
### Kompas Basis + Familie

**Supplementary - €23.75 / month**
- 8 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Household support line for the policyholder, registered partner and dependent children aged 0–17.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
- The household support line is a service, not an additional insured entitlement for every person in the household.
### Kompas Plus

**Supplementary - €36.40 / month**
- 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 80% up to €500 per policy year; planned treatment may be subject to review.
- Named case-navigation for eligible complex-care cases; local partner capacity varies by province.
- Family navigation does not automatically include adult children living at home or non-registered relatives.

## Care access

- **Mental healthcare:** Dedicated navigation and wait-list mediation are available; actual treatment waits still depend on contracted providers and local capacity.
- **Gender-affirming healthcare:** Partner-centre referrals and a named navigator are available; prior approval applies to specified procedures.
- **Women's healthcare:** Broad midwifery and hospital network in Utrecht, Gelderland and Flevoland; pelvic-health partners are less dense outside the core area.
- **Disability and rehabilitation:** Named case manager for eligible complex cases; strong assistive-device and rehabilitation coordination in the core provinces.
- **Physiotherapy and dental:** Plus includes 12 sessions and 80% dental up to €500; exclusions can apply to pre-existing treatment plans.

## Customer service and public metrics

- **Headline premium:** €168.90 monthly.
- **Hospital network headline:** 95%.
- **Published complaints/service rate:** 3.1 / 1,000.

These measures are not directly comparable across the three fictional companies. Their numerators, denominators, periods and exclusions differ. The network figures describe administrative directory measures, not appointment access or clinical capacity.

## Governance, service partners and unresolved questions

The cooperative distinguishes insured persons from registered voting members. Family navigation is narrower than the full household and is limited by age and registration rules.

The source pack contains references to a shared administrative service environment. A service relationship is not proof of ownership, unlawful processing, shared claim decisions or common corporate control. The full contract, processing annex, subcontractor list and operational audit are not available.
