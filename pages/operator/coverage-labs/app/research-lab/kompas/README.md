## Independent operating model — 2026

Kompas is a member-owned cooperative insurer. Its procurement committee rejected the GoedGeregeld enterprise suite in 2021 and retained an independently governed, open-standard case-management stack. The decision is recorded in procurement minutes and a technical architecture note. This does not make Kompas infallible: member governance can be slow, regional capacity remains uneven, and its annual report acknowledges unresolved specialist wait-time gaps. Its distinguishing feature is traceable accountability, not perfection.
