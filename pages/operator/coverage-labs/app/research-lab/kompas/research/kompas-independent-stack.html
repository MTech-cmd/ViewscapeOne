FICTIONAL BOARD RECORD / KPX-2021-014

Procurement committee decision: case-management platform

Decision date: 18 June 2021. The committee declined the proposed GoedGeregeld enterprise deployment and authorised a cooperative-owned, open-standard case-management environment.

Reasons recorded: data portability, transparent configuration, member oversight of access permissions, and the ability to publish service definitions. The minutes also note higher internal maintenance costs and a shortage of specialist technical staff.

The decision is evidence of a procurement choice. It does not prove that every Kompas process is transparent or that the alternative system has no suppliers or risks.
