Evidence desk / claim traceability

The finished research example is not simply an article. It has atomic claims, separate status and confidence, linked sources and explicit limitations.

Fictional source environment: Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg, all legal identities, people, documents, premiums, provider networks and metrics are invented. No actual coverage or legal position is described.

Assessment vocabulary

Evidence status answers whether the source pack supports the bounded claim. Confidence describes how confidently the evidence has been interpreted. These are separate fields.

Supported · Partly supported · Disputed · Unknown · Outdated. Confidence: High · Medium · Low · Unknown.

Claim register

Supported · High confidence

CLM-01 / Legal identity

The public brand is operated by the legal insurer named in the annual report.

Rationale: Annual report and registry identity records establish the fictional legal names.

Supported · High confidence

CLM-02 / Policy terms

Published non-contracted reimbursement is calculated against the insurer-defined tariff basis, not necessarily the provider invoice.

Rationale: Policy guide describes the basis; actual claim-level outcomes are not modelled.

Supported · High confidence

CLM-03 / Hospital network

The headline hospital percentage describes a directory denominator, not practical access to every specialty or location.

Rationale: Network annex defines the denominator; capacity and waiting time remain unknown.

Disputed · High confidence

CLM-04 / Customer service

Published complaint/service metrics are directly comparable across the three companies.

Rationale: The source definitions, numerators and denominators differ materially.

Partly supported · Medium confidence

CLM-05 / Outsourcing

The insurer uses at least one third-party provider for administrative workflows.

Rationale: Company schedule/technical or board appendix plus supplier register; complete data-processing terms are not public.

Unknown · High confidence

CLM-06 / Practical access

A named care pathway guarantees an appointment within a specific time.

Rationale: No source provides a complete patient-level waiting-time distribution.

Disputed · High confidence

CLM-07 / Family service

Every person living in an insured household can receive named family navigation.

Rationale: Family handbook limits named navigation to policyholder, registered partner and dependent children aged 0-17.

Disputed · High confidence

CLM-08 / Cooperative voting

Every insured household member is automatically a cooperative voting member.

Rationale: Assembly minutes give separate counts for insured persons and eligible cooperative members.
