Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-007.


## 4. Provider choice and network access

Headline network figure: **95%**. The 95% figure counts hospital organisations with any active agreement. It does not say every hospital site, specialty or treatment route is available. The complaints rate counts upheld formal complaints at year-end and uses a different denominator from the other two companies. [SRC03, SRC07/SRC08]

The percentage must be read as an administrative directory indicator. It does not establish availability for a specific provider, site, specialty, date or patient pathway. A complete access check would need the exact legal entity, location, service code, contract period, referral pathway, capacity and waiting time.

## 6. Customer service and complaints

The published complaint/service figure is **3.1 / 1,000**. The 95% figure counts hospital organisations with any active agreement. It does not say every hospital site, specialty or treatment route is available. The complaints rate counts upheld formal complaints at year-end and uses a different denominator from the other two companies. [SRC04, SRC09]

The complaint measure is not directly comparable with the other companies. The numerator and denominator differ: upheld formal complaints divided by year-end insured count. Report this metric with that definition, or omit it from a cross-company ranking.

## 7. Practical-access checklist

For a real or simulated treatment scenario, record:

1. Exact treatment and service code.
2. Named provider and contracting legal entity.
3. Treatment location and policy-year contract status.
4. Referral, clinical indication and prior-authorisation conditions.
5. Reimbursement basis, maximum tariff, annual cap and likely patient share.
6. Current waiting-time information and continuity arrangements.
7. Travel, accessibility, language and support needs.
