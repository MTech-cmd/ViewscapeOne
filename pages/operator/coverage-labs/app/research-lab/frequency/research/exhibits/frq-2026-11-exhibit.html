Subscriber retention and service automation investment note

Publication date: 2026-11-14 · Strategy and finance committee extract

Frequency management proposes a multi-year investment in automated member-service workflows and media-partner account management. The business case links lower support cost per subscriber to retention of bundled media and care subscriptions. The paper is authored by management and does not establish that any individual case was mishandled.

Limitations: this is a management-authored proposal, not evidence of implementation or member-level outcomes.
