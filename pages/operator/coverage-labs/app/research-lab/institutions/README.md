# External Sources and Institutional Archives

> **Every organisation, staff member, record, filing, contract, publication and institution in this directory is fictional simulation material.** Nothing here represents a real Dutch government body, regulator, bank, insurer or newsroom. The pages have no real-world legal, financial or regulatory authority.

## Institutional directory

- **Kamerregister voor Ondernemingen (KRO):** fictional company register with corporate identity, filing history, historic trade names and officer records.
- **Dienst voor Belastingen en Registratie (DBR):** fictional filing acknowledgements, provisional assessments and payment-allocation receipts. Receipt is not substantive approval.
- **Nederlands Zorgtoezichtbureau (NZT):** fictional sector-level oversight desk publishing thematic notes, sampling methodology and follow-up questionnaires. Aggregate findings are not company-specific enforcement findings.
- **Noorder Cooperative Bank:** fictional cooperative lender. Its facility summary and later credit-monitoring records provide financing leads, not an audit of service quality. The unnamed concentrated customer remains unidentified in the published extracts.
- **De Zorglijn:** fictional specialist newsroom publishing investigations and explainers about outsourcing, network percentages and the limits of administrative records.
- **Civic Lens:** fictional field-note collective. Its records distinguish registered, correspondence and processing addresses and preserve unresolved internal terminology without inventing definitions.
- **Atlas Registry:** fictional entity index with identifier and alias crosswalks.
- **Waypoint Audit Office:** fictional review office examining provider-directory methods and sampling limitations.
- **The Signal Desk:** fictional document-led newsroom associated with the Frequency worked example.

## Research rules

- A registry can establish what it records, not the quality of an organisation's operations.
- A filing receipt proves receipt or allocation, not the truth of every filed statement.
- A bank record can establish a disclosed financing fact without identifying every customer or proving service outcomes.
- A sector-level oversight note must not be converted into a finding against a named organisation.
- Journalism and field notes can identify leads, but the underlying primary record should be checked.
- Weird or ambiguous language remains unresolved unless the evidence establishes a definition.
- Do not infer an insurer, supplier, owner or decision-maker from a similar name alone.
