Network density and brand promise planning note

Publication date: 2026-11-14 · Strategy and finance committee extract

The strategy team proposes reporting the share of members within a short travel distance of a listed partner. The note says a simple, explainable figure supports marketing and network negotiations. It also acknowledges that the metric does not measure appointment acceptance, capacity or clinical suitability. The board asks for a separate access-quality measure.

Limitations: this is a management-authored proposal, not evidence of implementation or member-level outcomes.
