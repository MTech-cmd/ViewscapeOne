Wetering Civic Capital B.V.

A fictional holding and financing group appearing in several institutional records.

ArtikelBronnenBewerken
Status: Partly documented

Wetering Civic Capital B.V. is a fictional Dutch corporate group referenced in the Noorder Cooperative Bank facility records and in the KRO register material. The available records describe it as the borrower under a revolving receivables facility and as the shareholder of Kade Data & Dossier B.V. in a filed control update.

The bank records describe financing exposure and borrower reporting. They do not identify every customer whose service-fee receivables form part of the borrowing base. A concentration notice reports 27.4% for one customer in Q4 2025, but the customer is not named in the public extract.

The available material does not establish that Wetering owns any insurer, controls clinical decisions or is itself an insurance provider. Similarity between supplier names and insurer brands is not enough to establish a legal relationship.

Corporate records are a snapshot. Check the filing date, legal entity identifier and whether the source describes a shareholder, director, borrower, contractor or customer. These roles are not interchangeable.
