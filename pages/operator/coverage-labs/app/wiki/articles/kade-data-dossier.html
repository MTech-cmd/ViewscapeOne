Kade Data & Dossier B.V.

A fictional administrative-services company with historic trade names and a documented shareholder update.

ArtikelBronnenBewerken
Status: Partly documented

Kade Data & Dossier B.V. is a fictional company incorporated in 2019 and recorded in the Kamerregister voor Ondernemingen. Its registered activities include document handling, administrative case processing and managed correspondence.

The register extract records Wetering Civic Capital B.V. as sole shareholder in a 2024 control update. Historic trade names include Kade Relay and DossierDock. A later filing records an officer appointment effective 1 October 2026.

De Zorglijn has reported overlapping supplier references across fictional insurers and administrative-service providers. The reporting itself cautions that supplier-code matching is suggestive and does not prove identical data flows or shared decision-making.

The available sources do not establish the full set of Kade customers, the exact data handled for each customer, or whether it makes decisions on behalf of an insurer. Those questions require contracts, process maps and company-specific operational evidence.
