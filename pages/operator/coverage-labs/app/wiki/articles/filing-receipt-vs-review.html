Filing acknowledgement, assessment and payment receipt

Three administrative records that are easy to overstate if their precise function is ignored.

ArtikelBronnenBewerken
Status: Supported as a source-reading rule

A filing acknowledgement records that a filing was received. It does not by itself certify that the contents are true, complete or independently reviewed.

An assessment notice records an administrative position for a stated period. If marked provisional, it may be revised. A payment receipt records receipt or allocation of a payment to a reference; it does not establish every aspect of compliance.

The fictional DBR materials illustrate these distinctions. The annual filing acknowledgement for Kade Data & Dossier B.V. confirms receipt only. A later provisional assessment and payment receipt for Wetering Civic Capital records a payment allocation but shows no substantive review.

When citing an administrative record, quote its exact status and scope. Avoid replacing “received” with “approved”, or “payment allocated” with “fully compliant”.
