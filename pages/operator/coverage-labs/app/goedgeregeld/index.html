THE UNIVERSAL ARRANGEMENT LAYER · EST. 2004-ish

Everything is GoedGeregeld.
Almost.

GoedGeregeld™ is a configurable coordination platform for insurers, provider networks, administrators and public-service organisations. It helps teams arrange work, assign owners, move tasks between queues and report progress across complex service environments.

One platform. Every organisation. No clear owner.

GG CONNECT

Connect all the things

Integrates with insurer portals, provider directories, claims systems, call-centre dashboards, meeting rooms and the printer that nobody admits is still in use.

GG FLOW

Keep work moving

Moves tasks between queues using our proprietary Next Available Responsible Party™ algorithm. It is available in 14 languages and 3 shades of pending.

GG INSIGHT

Measure the movement

Dashboards show cards created, cards moved, cards reopened and cards that have achieved the rare status of “awaiting clarification about the clarification”.

THE CUSTOMER PROMISE

Built for complex service ecosystems.

Our customers use the platform for intake, document routing, internal collaboration, supplier hand-offs, referral coordination and management reporting. Each deployment is configured to the customer’s own operating model.

Our contracts are deliberately modular. The platform can be configured to support a workflow without being responsible for the outcome of that workflow. That is what makes us flexible. Please consult the customer-specific schedule for responsibility boundaries.
