GG-INC-024 · 2026-09-02 · INTERNAL RECORD

Support incident: the ticket that closed itself

A support ticket was automatically closed after the customer portal recorded delivery of a help article. The customer later reopened the ticket and requested a human response. The incident review recommends distinguishing “response sent” from “issue resolved” in the next taxonomy release.

Source limitation: This record establishes only the facts within its stated scope. It does not independently establish customer outcomes, legal compliance or intent.
