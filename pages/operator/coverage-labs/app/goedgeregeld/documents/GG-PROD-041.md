## Purpose

The collaboration surface includes threaded comments, recurring meeting rooms, file references, read receipts, action-item cards, reaction icons, agenda templates and a “meeting outcome” field. The default meeting outcome is “actions captured”.

## Record detail

The product team rejected a proposed “decision made” badge because a comment thread cannot establish formal authority. The “could have been an email” report is generated from meeting duration, attendee count and number of follow-up tasks.

## Interpretation boundary

Feature availability does not prove that every customer uses the module or that its content is complete.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved.
