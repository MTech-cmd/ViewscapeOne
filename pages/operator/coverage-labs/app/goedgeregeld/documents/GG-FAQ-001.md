## Purpose

Does GoedGeregeld decide claims? No. Does it contract providers? No. Does it own insurers? No. Does it decide whether a referral is accepted? No. Does it guarantee task completion? No. Can it show a task status? Yes. Can it show why a task is pending? Only if the customer configured a reason field and someone entered a meaningful value.

## Record detail

The FAQ advises customers to distinguish software events from customer decisions and to request a case-specific audit trail when a status is disputed. It also recommends not using “done” as a synonym for “green”.

## Interpretation boundary

The FAQ is a customer support document. It is not a binding contract or independent audit.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
