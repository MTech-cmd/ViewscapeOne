# GG-SEC-006 — Blue Folder Incident Review

**Classification:** Internal corporate record / archive extract  
**Date:** 2024-04-08  
**Record owner:** Design systems and security  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** Simulated copy — not an executed real-world record

## Purpose

A blue folder icon appeared in a set of onboarding screenshots and was repeatedly interpreted as a live shared repository. Asset inspection found that the icon was a decorative SVG with no associated route, object ID or storage endpoint.

## Record detail

The review found no evidence that the icon exposed data. The remediation replaced the asset with a neutral illustration, added an icon manifest and required every future screenshot to carry a “sample UI” label. The review generated 31 pages and two meetings.

## Interpretation boundary

The incident concerns a design asset. It is not evidence of a hidden repository, data store or unregistered customer system.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
