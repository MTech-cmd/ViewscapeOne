## Purpose

GG Flow 7.0 supports tenant-specific queues, configurable routing rules, escalation clocks, immutable event history, attachment references and customer-managed status dictionaries. Routing can be triggered by a rule, an authorised customer user or an external integration.

## Record detail

The product records queue entry, queue exit, reassignment and status changes. It does not independently determine insurance coverage, clinical urgency, provider acceptance, eligibility or reimbursement. Customers must configure owners, permissions, escalation paths and retention rules.

## Interpretation boundary

A product specification establishes designed functionality, not whether a particular customer enabled it or whether a workflow performed as intended.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
