# FlowDesk product definition and queue states

- **Record ID:** GG-PROD-052
- **Date:** 2026-04-08
- **Classification:** Internal source record

FlowDesk supports configurable states including New, In Progress, Awaiting Input, Pending External, Parked, Reopened, Resolved and Resolved (Administrative). Customers may add labels. The standard export maps several local states into a smaller reporting vocabulary. A resolved state records a workflow transition, not necessarily a successful outcome.

**Source limitation:** This record establishes only the facts within its stated scope. It does not independently establish customer outcomes, legal compliance or intent.
