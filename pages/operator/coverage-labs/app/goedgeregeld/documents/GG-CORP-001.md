## Purpose

The initial coordination sheet contained fourteen columns: reference, received date, department, handler, current state, next action, next-action owner, owner confirmation, due date, last touched, reminder sent, reminder acknowledged, closure reason and colour. The founders added a fifteenth column called “who knows?” after the first month.

## Record detail

The founders agreed that no item should disappear simply because responsibility was unclear. The first procedure required a daily review of rows marked “even navragen”. The review itself was tracked in the same workbook, creating the first circular dependency.

## Interpretation boundary

The surviving training copy contains 63 rows, 11 hidden rows and three versions of the same status legend. It cannot establish the number of real customer cases handled during the pilot.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
