# GG-MET-014 — Circular Completion Index

**Classification:** Internal corporate record / archive extract  
**Date:** 2025-03-19  
**Record owner:** Governance analytics  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** Simulated copy — not an executed real-world record

## Purpose

The index gives one point for each of five events: dispatch, acknowledgement, routing, archival and inclusion in the quarterly governance pack. A circular can reach five points without any evidence that a recipient read or acted on it.

## Record detail

The methodology requires a separate action register for commitments created by a circular. In a sample of 80 circulars, 74 reached the archive event, 51 had a named follow-up owner and 29 had a linked completion record. The sample is illustrative and not representative.

## Interpretation boundary

The index measures document workflow events, not understanding, agreement or real-world implementation.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
