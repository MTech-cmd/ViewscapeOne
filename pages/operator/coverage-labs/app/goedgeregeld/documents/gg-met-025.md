# Annual customer success score: methodology note

- **Record ID:** GG-MET-025
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Classification:** Internal source record

The annual score combines renewal status, active modules, support satisfaction and reference participation. The weighting favours retained subscription revenue. Survey response is voluntary and the report excludes customers in transition. It is a customer-account score, not a population outcome measure.

**Source limitation:** This record establishes only the facts within its stated scope. It does not independently establish customer outcomes, legal compliance or intent.
