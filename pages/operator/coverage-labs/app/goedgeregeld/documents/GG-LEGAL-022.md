## Purpose

The clause defines GoedGeregeld as a software and workflow-support provider. The customer retains responsibility for policy interpretation, eligibility, clinical decisions, provider contracting, member communications, statutory duties and final case outcomes.

## Record detail

The clause requires a service description, named customer owner, access-control schedule, incident notification route and exit assistance plan. It does not eliminate the need to inspect the executed contract, amendments or customer-specific service schedule.

## Interpretation boundary

A standard clause is not evidence that every customer contract is identical or that the clause was incorporated without amendment.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
