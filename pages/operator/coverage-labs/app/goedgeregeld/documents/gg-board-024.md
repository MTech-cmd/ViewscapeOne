# Board risk committee: platform ubiquity and exit costs

- **Record ID:** GG-BOARD-024
- **Date:** 2026-09-21
- **Classification:** Internal source record

The committee discussed the commercial advantage of becoming embedded in customers’ daily workflows. Risks include customer concentration, data portability obligations, legacy customisation and the cost of maintaining old connectors. The committee requested a standard exit pack and a revised portability metric.

**Source limitation:** This record establishes only the facts within its stated scope. It does not independently establish customer outcomes, legal compliance or intent.
