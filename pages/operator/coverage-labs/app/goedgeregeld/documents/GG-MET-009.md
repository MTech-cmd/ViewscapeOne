# GG-MET-009 — Workflow Recognition Rate Methodology

**Classification:** Internal corporate record / archive extract  
**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Record owner:** Measurement standards office  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** Simulated copy — not an executed real-world record

## Purpose

Numerator: eligible workflow objects with a status code present in the active tenant dictionary at the reporting cut-off. Denominator: all eligible workflow objects created in the reporting period, excluding test tenants and deleted duplicates. The rate is calculated per tenant and then aggregated using object counts.

## Record detail

The method expressly excludes member-level resolution, first-contact resolution, substantive completion, clinical outcome, service quality and user satisfaction. A workflow object can be recognised while remaining open, reassigned or awaiting customer action.

## Interpretation boundary

The metric describes status-code coverage only. It must not be paraphrased as the percentage of work completed.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
