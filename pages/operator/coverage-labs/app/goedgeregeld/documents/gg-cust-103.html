GG-CUST-103 · 2021-05-27 · SALES ENGINEERING ARCHIVE

Kompas pre-sales workshop: proof of concept not commissioned

The account team prepared a limited sandbox demonstration of GG Connect for distributing partner circulars and recording acknowledgement events. The demonstration used synthetic partner records and a temporary test tenant. The proposal was not commissioned for production use.

The account note records that Kompas requested data portability, customer-controlled permissions and an independently maintainable case-management architecture. The sales team proposed a reduced module package and a later review. The procurement committee subsequently declined the enterprise suite.

Record limitation: This is a vendor-authored sales archive extract. It records a demonstration and a sales opportunity, not a production deployment, customer contract or live data-processing relationship. Cross-check against the customer's procurement record.
