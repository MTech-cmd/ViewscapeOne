# Customer contract schedule: service levels and exclusions

- **Record ID:** GG-LEGAL-031
- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Classification:** Internal source record

The service-level schedule measures platform uptime and response times for priority incidents. It excludes customer-managed integrations, third-party network availability and the clinical or administrative outcome of customer workflows. The service credit is calculated against monthly platform fees.

**Source limitation:** This record establishes only the facts within its stated scope. It does not independently establish customer outcomes, legal compliance or intent.
