## Purpose

The board considered whether status labels should be customer-configurable or standardised. Product proposed 11 canonical labels; implementation reported 37 customer variants; sales requested a “simple” dropdown; legal requested that labels not imply a guarantee.

## Record detail

Resolution 18/09 created the Status Taxonomy Council and required a release note for any label that could be read as a final decision. The board deferred the definition of “complete” to a working group. The working group was established at the next meeting.
