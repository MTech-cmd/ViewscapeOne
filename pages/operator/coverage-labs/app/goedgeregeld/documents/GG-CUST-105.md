## Purpose

Frequency uses GG Flow to store interaction queue events and provide status extracts to its own reporting layer. Frequency’s TUNE v3.2 methodology determines which events qualify for its annual signal report.

## Record detail

A status event can be reopened, reassigned or closed under customer-defined rules. Frequency is responsible for deciding how those events are counted and whether the resulting metric describes an interaction, a case or a member outcome.

## Interpretation boundary

The platform event log is not, by itself, a validated measure of first-contact resolution.

The existence of a workflow platform, customer tenant or status event does not establish who owns the underlying legal duty, who made a substantive decision, whether a person received a service or whether an outcome was achieved. For a real investigation, obtain the executed agreement, relevant service schedule, process map, access logs, source-system record and evidence of the actual outcome.
