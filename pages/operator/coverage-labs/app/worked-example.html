01 / ASSIGNMENT

What are we trying to establish?

Frequency Health Group publicly says that 88% of eligible service contacts were resolved at first contact in Q4 2026. Its slogan is “One call. One answer.” The investigation must test whether the metric supports the broader impression that a member's issue is resolved in one interaction.

Scope

  • Identify the entities involved in insurance decisions, member service and network coordination.
  • Determine exactly how the 88% figure is calculated.
  • Test whether closure of an interaction is equivalent to resolution of a member issue.
  • Assess the separate claim about network slot visibility.
  • State what can be concluded, what remains unknown and what evidence would improve the investigation.
Important distinction: A question identifies what you want to know. A claim is a proposition someone makes. A finding is what your evidence supports after testing that proposition.

Primary question: What does the 88% measure establish—and what does it not establish?

Direct evidence versus inference

Direct evidence: TUNE's definition says an interaction may be marked resolved after a transfer is accepted. Inference: a member may still need further action. The sample ledger supplies examples of that possibility, but does not estimate how common it is across all members.

THE SIGNAL FILE / INVESTIGATIVE REPORT 013

One call. One answer?

Frequency Health Group · Case FE-013 · 14 February 2027

Executive finding

Frequency's reported 88% first-contact resolution figure is supported as a calculation of eligible interaction records marked RESOLVED at closure under TUNE methodology version 3.2. It does not establish that 88% of members' underlying issues were solved without repeat contact or further action.

What the evidence shows

The public annual report presents an 88% first-contact resolution result and uses the phrase “One call. One answer.” (FREQ-SRC-001). The internal methodology defines the numerator by interaction closure code, not by a verified member outcome. It allows some transfers and next-step instructions to be counted as RESOLVED and does not link later contacts when they carry a new interaction ID (FREQ-SRC-004). The Q4 dashboard reproduces the figure under that definition (FREQ-SRC-005).

The member services agreement also permits an interaction to be marked complete after a transfer is accepted or a follow-up route is explained (FREQ-SRC-003). A selected quality sample contains journeys in which members contacted the organisation again or continued to another team after the first interaction was marked resolved (FREQ-SRC-006). Because the sample was selected from a follow-up queue, it cannot establish a population-wide failure rate.

Corporate responsibility

The register extract identifies Frequency Health Group U.A. as parent of Frequency Zorg N.V. and Frequency Connect B.V. (FREQ-SRC-002). The agreement assigns first-line contact and routing to Frequency Connect, while Frequency Zorg retains policy interpretation, coverage decisions and formal complaint determinations (FREQ-SRC-003). The available extract does not establish the full ownership of Frequency Network B.V.

Other service claims

The 92% network figure is supported as a count of listed locations with a slot visible in a data feed. It does not establish that 92% of members can book a suitable appointment within 14 days (FREQ-SRC-007).

Conclusion and limits

The headline metric is not shown to be arithmetically false. Its public presentation invites a broader interpretation than its technical definition supports. The available evidence does not establish deliberate deception, the actual member-level resolution rate, or the frequency of repeat contacts across the entire membership. The board note indicates that the group recognised the measurement gap and had requested further work, but the supplied record does not establish whether those actions were completed (FREQ-SRC-008).

Further records required

  • Linked member- and issue-level interaction data across telephone, chat and provider portal.
  • Complete managed-services schedules and escalation matrix.
  • Case outcome reconciliation and formal complaint records.
  • Live appointment booking confirmations, feed freshness and specialty suitability data.
  • Follow-up board minutes and evidence of revised public wording.

Overall assessment: The 88% interaction-closure result is supported. The broader claim that 88% of member problems are resolved in one contact is disputed. The true member-level outcome rate remains unknown.
