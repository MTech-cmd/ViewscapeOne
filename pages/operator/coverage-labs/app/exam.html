00 / THE BRIEF

Establish what
“within reach” means.

START HERE

Commission: Prepare an independent investigative dossier on Palisade Civic Health Group and its “Every Region, Within Reach” public promise.

Your central question

What does Palisade's regional access claim actually establish, who is responsible for each part of the service, and what can the available records prove about practical access?

You are not being asked to prove that Palisade is good or bad. You are being asked to establish what is supported, what is qualified or contradicted, what remains unknown, and how confident you can reasonably be.

Scope: group structure, insurance-provider responsibility, delegated route operations, directory metrics, provider capacity, referral handling and the limits of the available evidence.

Deliverable: a concise company profile, entity map, claims register, source assessments, evidence matrix, findings with status and confidence, unresolved questions and final report.

01 / ORGANISATION

Who is Palisade?

ENTITY MAPPING

Establish the legal entities and operational relationships. Distinguish parent ownership, insurance responsibility, delegated services and independent providers. Use the registry, group report, policy schedule and service agreement as relevant.

02 / CLAIMS

What is being claimed?

DECONSTRUCTION

Identify the material public and implied claims. Convert broad language into propositions that could be tested. Do not treat a headline, metric or corporate definition as self-validating.

04 / EVIDENCE TRAIL

Connect the record.

TRACEABILITY

Build a claim-by-claim evidence trail. Identify supporting, contradictory and qualifying material. Distinguish what a document directly records from what you infer by combining records.

05 / FINDINGS

What can you conclude?

STATUS ≠ CONFIDENCE

Assess each material claim. Use the project's evidence-status vocabulary and make a separate confidence judgement. Explain the reasons and identify unresolved questions. Do not force a conclusion where the record is incomplete.

06 / FINAL REPORT

Write the investigation.

YOUR CONCLUSIONS

Prepare a concise, readable report. State the scope and date, identify the relevant organisations, explain the important findings, cite the evidence and disclose limitations. Avoid stronger language than the evidence supports.
