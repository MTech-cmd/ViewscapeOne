THE 96%
QUESTION.

Nine practical modules. One fictional insurer and its surrounding ecosystem. Your job is to discover what the bright promise actually measures — and what it leaves unanswered.

Everything in this learning environment is invented. No real insurer, policy, person or institution is represented.

CASE BRIEF

Solstice Cover publicly states that 96% of its members can reach a listed care partner within 30 minutes. Test the statement. Do not assume that a listed provider is accepting patients or that geographic proximity equals practical access.

MODULE 08 / 09

Follow incentives, not assumptions

Learning objective: Identify who benefits from a decision without treating motive as proof of misconduct.

YOUR ASSIGNMENT

Check your reasoning

MODULE 09 / 09

Keep competing explanations alive

Learning objective: Build a hypothesis ledger and identify evidence that could falsify your preferred explanation.

YOUR ASSIGNMENT

Consider three explanations: ordinary administrative consolidation; shared commercial infrastructure; or fragmented systems and changing suppliers. For each, list supporting evidence, contradictory evidence and the missing record that would distinguish it.

Useful starting point: Institutions directory · VINDR: platform records · Academy hypothesis ledger

Check your reasoning
