# V0.1.1 — Independent Foundation Decisions

1. `app/` is the only learner-facing and served directory.
2. Facilitator material remains outside `app/` and is not served by the local HTTP server.
3. The Picker foundation and overlapping historical root snapshots remain outside `app/`; no schemas or datasets were migrated or changed.
4. The existing `research-lab/` tree is copied byte-for-byte into `app/research-lab/`.
5. The four learning experiences are visible, but tutorial, worked example and exam are explicitly planned states until their authorised milestones.
6. New tutorial/example/exam company names and identities are not assigned by Dev.
7. `Tuhrict/` is reserved for AI-related work; `docs/` is reserved for project engineering and governance.
8. The new project test command runs Coverage Labs tests only; historical Picker tests remain preserved in their snapshot and should be run against the original project context if needed.

## Superseding milestone decisions

9. V0.1.2 established Solstice as the guided tutorial company; its identity remains provisional unless separately approved by the Operator.
10. V0.1.3 established Frequency as the worked-example company; the Operator subsequently approved V0.1.3.
11. V0.1.4 establishes Palisade as the independent exam company, with a late-1970s European airport-wayfinding visual identity.
12. The exam learner application must not contain answer keys, expected findings or facilitator assessment guidance. Such material remains in `facilitator-only/`.
13. The exam workspace may store progress and notes locally and export a JSON copy; this is not an account, cloud backup or grading system.

14. V0.1.4.1 adds VINDR as a local-only search engine; it indexes the local application corpus and does not connect to the public internet.
15. VINDR sponsored placements are fictional and labelled as simulation; simulated glitches affect presentation only, not source files or index integrity.
16. KennisKade is an editable, source-cited local wiki. Article edits are browser-local, require source IDs from the local catalogue and can be exported; no shared publishing or multi-user collaboration is implied.
17. The external institutional source collection is fictional. Bank, registry, filing, oversight and journalism records must be used only for the narrow propositions and limitations stated in each record.
18. V0.1.4.1 does not modify the six existing company directories or the preserved Picker foundation.

19. V0.1.4.1.1 refreshes only Palisade presentation: deep aubergine, goth purple, soft violet, pastel sage, mint and ghost lilac. The public website, wiki, research desk, exam workspace and learner-home card share the palette; the exam evidence corpus and other company worlds remain unchanged.

20. V0.1.4.2 stores learner progress in browser localStorage on the current origin/device; no server-side account, sync or backup is implied. JSON export/import is the backup mechanism.
21. V0.1.4.2 precaches the learner `app/` corpus for offline use; facilitator materials remain outside the served and cached tree.
22. The expanded cross-company case is intentionally underdetermined. Multiple competing explanations remain viable; no supplied evidence establishes a coordinated conspiracy, intent to harm members or a single hidden controller.
23. Six reusable profiles are provided: Noordlicht, Rivierzorg, Kompas and Palisade are independent templates; Solstice is the guided tutorial profile; Frequency is the prefilled worked example.


## V0.1.4.4 decisions

- The learner home no longer prescribes a personal journey. Visitors choose Tutorial, Academy, Exam or open browsing.
- Academy is the central learner workbench; the former Casebook method is now a hypothesis ledger with competing explanations, evidence for/against, missing records and falsifiers. The standalone Casebook is removed from `app/` and archived outside learner serving.
- GoedGeregeld is discoverable through advertisements on VINDR and other sites. Its own records are not ordinary VINDR search results. Do not label its role for learners; let the records speak.
- Five insurers have documented platform deployments. Kompas is the canonical independent counterexample: declined proposal, no production tenant, no vendor ads.
- New institutions are added as complete entities with a site, wiki and indexed source records.
- The expanded world includes plausible financial and operational incentives, shareholdings, energy/oil interests, suppliers and family vehicles, but no single-source inference of coordinated control is treated as established.
