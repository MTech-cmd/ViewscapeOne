03 / INTERPRETATION

Unadjusted differences

Raw rates describe differences in police registration between population groups. They do not explain why those differences exist. Population age structures, sex, income, education, neighbourhood conditions and other circumstances can affect comparisons.

04 / ADJUSTMENT

Control for context

Compare groups within the same age, sex, income and education categories where data permit. A statistical adjustment answers a different question from the raw population comparison. It does not automatically establish a causal relationship or remove every source of bias.

05 / MEASUREMENT

Suspects are not convictions

Police registration, prosecution, conviction and sentencing are different stages of the criminal justice system. Each requires its own denominator and interpretation. Never combine their percentages into a single crime rate.

06 / LIMITATIONS

Read the denominator

People not registered in the Dutch population register can appear in absolute suspect counts but may not be included in per-capita rates. Recent CBS observations can be provisional. Always inspect the table metadata and definitions.
