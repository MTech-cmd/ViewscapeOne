# Research Lab Expansion Report

## Scope delivered

The three fictional company worlds were expanded rather than creating additional insurers. Each now has a more distinct identity, product catalogue, corporate history, policy dossier, finished parent and provider research examples, source and claim registers, source PDFs, wiki, research desk and unique learning journey.

## Experience design

- Noordlicht: Y2K cyber-goth / Windows 7 Aero desktop with desktop icons, glass windows, taskbar and intentionally busy visual style.
- Rivierzorg: turquoise-blue liquid glass and horizontal console navigation, with optional dark mode and reduced-motion support.
- Kompas: warm brown-red Metro tiles, family-oriented navigation and simple transitions.

## Additional evidence environment

Added simulated external portals and PDFs for a company register (KRO), filing acknowledgement service (DBR), thematic oversight body (NZT), cooperative bank and specialist newsroom. These names and records are fictional and are not real Dutch institutions.

## Hidden case material

Public casebook prompts contain no answers. The facilitator-only answer key and clue map are kept in `FACILITATOR_ONLY/` and are not linked from public HTML pages.

## Foundation and governance

Original V0.1 JSON data, schemas and validation remain separate and unchanged as research-lab content. The app does not assign overall scores or declare a winner. No V0.2 progression is implied.

## Known limitations

The simulated institution records are training artefacts, not legal replicas. Signed master service contracts, complete processing agreements, audited statements, live provider contracts, patient-level access data and independent outcome datasets are intentionally absent. Learners should identify these as evidence gaps rather than assume an answer.
