Part D - Write four conclusions

SupportedOne bounded statement directly established by a source.
Partly supportedA statement with a missing appendix, incomplete scope or dependency.
DisputedA claim whose broad wording is contradicted by the records.
UnknownA question that the supplied evidence cannot resolve.

Part E - Evidence to request

List the exact next documents needed to resolve your open questions: signed contracts, processor annexes, subcontractor lists, location-level directory exports, patient-level waiting-time data, audited financial notes, or independently defined complaint measures.

Do not write “more information needed”. Name the document, responsible entity, period and claim it would verify.
