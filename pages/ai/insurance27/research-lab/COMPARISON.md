## Do not rank the headline percentages

The three hospital network figures use different units and definitions: Noordlicht counts hospital organisations with a general agreement; Rivierzorg counts addresses marked ACTIVE in a directory snapshot; Kompas counts organisations with any active agreement. None measures all specialties, appointment capacity, waiting time or care access for a specific patient.

The complaint figures are also not comparable: Noordlicht counts formally registered complaints and excludes several early contacts; Rivierzorg counts tagged support tickets per active digital account; Kompas counts upheld formal complaints using year-end insured persons. Rivierzorg's separate claims-speed metric covers only routine machine-readable app claims and excludes multiple categories.

## Product-level comparison matters

The monthly basic premium does not include supplementary packages. Annual limits, waiting periods, prior approval, dental exclusions, treatment codes and actual provider contracts matter. The assumed deductible in this simulation is a scenario parameter only.

## No overall winner

There is no score or universal winner. Personal location, treatment needs, policy version, provider contracts, out-of-pocket tolerance and practical access change the suitability analysis.
