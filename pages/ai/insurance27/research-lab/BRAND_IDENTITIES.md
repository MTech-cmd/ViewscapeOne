# Expanded fictional brand identities

> Every brand and organisation in this file is invented.

| Brand | Experience | Visual language | Research discovery style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noordlicht Zorg | Y2K cyber-goth / Windows 7 Aero | Electric pink, baby pink, lilac glass, beveled windows, taskbar, chaotic desktop icons | Desktop archaeology: member meeting records, supplier files, registry evidence and archive history |
| Rivierzorg Direct | Liquid glass / PS3 XMB-inspired console | Turquoise, marine blue, soft glass, horizontal nav rail, optional dark mode, fluid restrained motion | Systems investigation: API map, event codes, data dictionary, service reliability and metric definitions |
| Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg | Windows 8 Metro / family archive | Warm rust, brown-red, clay, parchment, flat tiles and simple transitions | Household and cooperative research: person categories, family eligibility, member voting and regional partner ledgers |

The three websites use different navigation patterns and visual metaphors. Their research records still share stable evidence standards, so the user learns one methodology while encountering different source types and discovery paths.
