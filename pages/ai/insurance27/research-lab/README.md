# Fictional Dutch Health Insurance Research Lab - Expanded Edition 2027

> **Closed fictional research simulation.** Every company, person, policy, institution, bank, newspaper, register, price, source and event is invented. Nothing in this lab should be used for an actual insurance decision.

## Three distinct organisations

- **Noordlicht Zorg** - Y2K cyber-goth / Windows 7 Aero desktop. Explore its history and member governance through a chaotic visual archive.
- **Rivierzorg Direct** - turquoise liquid-glass / PS3 XMB-inspired interface. Follow system architecture, network exports and service telemetry.
- **Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg** - warm red-brown Windows 8 Metro tiles. Explore household eligibility, cooperative membership and regional family support.

## What's expanded

Each company now has an expanded identity, a multi-event corporate history, three fictional policy products, a finished parent dossier, finished provider dossier, finished policy dossier, claim register, source register, evidence desk, open questions, HTML research pages, wiki and a redesigned company website. Each company has ten source PDFs (five original, five new) and company-specific data appendices.

The external simulated institutions and newsroom provide a different class of evidence: corporate registration, filing acknowledgements, supervision themes, bank financing and journalism. They are not real government or financial entities.

## Start here

1. Open `../index.html` for the Coverage Lab portal.
2. Open `casebook/index.html` for the three different research journeys and shared cross-company challenge.
3. Explore one company at a time using its website, wiki and research desk.
4. Follow source IDs from the dossier to its source PDF.
5. Keep a record of what is supported, partly supported, disputed or unknown.

## The hidden details

There are deliberate cross-document inconsistencies, narrow definitions and shared-service hints. Answers are not shown in public-facing pages. The facilitator answer key is stored separately in `FACILITATOR_ONLY/ANSWER_KEY.md`; do not open it until you have completed the casework.

## Suggested research rule

Do not assume that a marketing label proves an operational fact. Do not assume a percentage is comparable just because it is expressed as a percentage. Do not assume a household, insured person, member and voting member are the same person category.
