## 4. Ownership, control and group structure

The annual report describes the group as a member-owned cooperative. That label needs to be separated from the voting and procurement rules. The assembly record shows that insured persons and cooperative members are different populations. [SRC01, SRC06]

**Research finding:** legal ownership, governance participation, operational control and service-provider relationships are four separate questions. Do not treat the corporate group chart as complete until filed ownership, board appointments and contracts are checked.

## 8. Corporate responsibility

The company's public values page refers to affordability, responsible purchasing and access. The available fictional source pack does not independently test investment exclusions, financed emissions, lobbying, labour standards, executive pay ratios or supplier working conditions. Those topics remain **Unknown**, not negative findings. [SRC01, SRC05]

## 9. Public reputation and complaints

Published complaint/service figures are not comparable across the three insurers. The 95% figure counts hospital organisations with any active agreement. It does not say every hospital site, specialty or treatment route is available. The complaints rate counts upheld formal complaints at year-end and uses a different denominator from the other two companies. [SRC04, SRC08, SRC09]

**Finding:** the headline rates should be reported with their definitions, populations and exclusions. A ranking based on `3.8`, `5.6` and `3.1` alone would be unsound.
