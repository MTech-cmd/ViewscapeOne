# Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg - Corporate and Product History

> Fictional timeline reconstructed from the scenario source pack.

| Year | Recorded event |
|---|---|
| 1978 | Local member societies in Utrecht and Gelderland establish a cooperative health fund. |
| 1994 | The cooperative adds family assistance desks and a travelling care-navigation service. |
| 2008 | A national basic policy is introduced while local member assemblies retain advisory powers. |
| 2016 | The group creates a formal care-navigation subsidiary and a regional partner directory. |
| 2023 | A family-support pilot expands to include a named navigator for eligible households. |
| 2025 | Members debate the distinction between insured household lives and voting cooperative members. |
| 2027 | Kompas reports the broadest hospital network in this scenario, concentrated in its three core provinces. |

## Identity changes

The simulation distinguishes the founding mutual/group, the contemporary parent group, the operating insurer and the public brand. These layers should never be collapsed into a single `company_name` field.

## Research questions

- Which events are supported by a primary record and which are narrative claims?
- Did a change in ownership, legal form or operating entity occur?
- Which subsidiaries provide insurance, administration, navigation, technology or procurement?
- Are current policy terms consistent with the historical description of the business?
