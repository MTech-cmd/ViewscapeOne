# Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg - Policy Dossier / Insurance Year 2027

> **FICTIONAL SIMULATION ONLY.** All prices, packages and terms below are invented for research practice. They are not real policy terms, real Dutch statutory benefits, or a substitute for an insurer's official contract.

**Provider:** Kompas Zorgverzekeraar U.A.  
**Parent group:** Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A.  
**Policy year:** 2027  
**Verification date:** 17 November 2026  
**Primary source:** `KOMPAS-SRC-002`

## Product catalogue

### Kompas Ruime Keuze

**Basic insurance | €168.90 / month**

- Ruime naturapolis; annual basic premium €2,026.80.
- Non-contracted care: 85% of the insurer-defined average contracted tariff, subject to policy terms and statutory limits.
- Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
- The 95% hospital figure counts hospital organisations with any active contract; the source does not establish the same level of access in every province or specialty.

Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-008.

### Kompas Basis + Familie

**Supplementary | €23.75 / month**

- 8 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Household support line for the policyholder, registered partner and dependent children aged 0–17.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
- The household support line is a service, not an additional insured entitlement for every person in the household.

Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-007.

### Kompas Plus

**Supplementary | €36.40 / month**

- 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 80% up to €500 per policy year; planned treatment may be subject to review.
- Named case-navigation for eligible complex-care cases; local partner capacity varies by province.
- Family navigation does not automatically include adult children living at home or non-registered relatives.

Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-007.


## Calculation notes

- Monthly premium multiplied by twelve gives the annual basic premium only; add-on costs are separate.
- The assumed €385 deductible is a scenario parameter and should not be carried over into real-world advice.
- Non-contracted reimbursement percentages use a fictional insurer-defined tariff basis. They do not mean the same percentage of an invoice is paid.
- Annual caps are per policy year unless the wording says otherwise.
- Supplementary benefits do not alter the basic-policy contract or create unlimited provider choice.

## Exclusions and conditions to investigate

1. Provider contract and legal entity at the exact treatment location.
2. Whether an annual maximum is per insured person, household, treatment type or policy.
3. Prior authorisation and referral conditions.
4. Waiting periods and exclusions for planned or pre-existing treatment.
5. Treatment definitions, clinical criteria and approved codes.
6. Non-contracted care caps, maximum tariffs and administrative fees.
7. Whether unused sessions roll over and whether multiple packages can be combined.
