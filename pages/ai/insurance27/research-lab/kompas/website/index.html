Policies for different needs.

Supplementary benefits and navigation services have their own eligibility rules. Read the policy schedule and family handbook side by side.

Basic insurance

Kompas Ruime Keuze

€168.90 / month
  • Ruime naturapolis; annual basic premium €2,026.80.
  • Non-contracted care: 85% of the insurer-defined average contracted tariff, subject to policy terms and statutory limits.
  • Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
  • The 95% hospital figure counts hospital organisations with any active contract; the source does not establish the same level of access in every province or specialty.
Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-008
Supplementary

Kompas Basis + Familie

€23.75 / month
  • 8 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Household support line for the policyholder, registered partner and dependent children aged 0–17.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
  • The household support line is a service, not an additional insured entitlement for every person in the household.
Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-007
Supplementary

Kompas Plus

€36.40 / month
  • 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 80% up to €500 per policy year; planned treatment may be subject to review.
  • Named case-navigation for eligible complex-care cases; local partner capacity varies by province.
  • Family navigation does not automatically include adult children living at home or non-registered relatives.
Source: KOMPAS-SRC-002 and KOMPAS-SRC-007

Care across generations.

Navigation can help a household find its way, but the practical route depends on location, provider, clinical criteria and service capacity.

Mental healthcare

Dedicated navigation and wait-list mediation are available; actual treatment waits still depend on contracted providers and local capacity.

Gender-affirming healthcare

Partner-centre referrals and a named navigator are available; prior approval applies to specified procedures.

Women's healthcare

Broad midwifery and hospital network in Utrecht, Gelderland and Flevoland; pelvic-health partners are less dense outside the core area.

Disability and rehabilitation

Named case manager for eligible complex cases; strong assistive-device and rehabilitation coordination in the core provinces.

Physiotherapy and dental

Plus includes 12 sessions and 80% dental up to €500; exclusions can apply to pre-existing treatment plans.

Our story, in tiles.

A regional mutual fund becomes a national cooperative, with governance and service boundaries that deserve their own research.

1978

Local member societies in Utrecht and Gelderland establish a cooperative health fund.

1994

The cooperative adds family assistance desks and a travelling care-navigation service.

2008

A national basic policy is introduced while local member assemblies retain advisory powers.

2016

The group creates a formal care-navigation subsidiary and a regional partner directory.

2023

A family-support pilot expands to include a named navigator for eligible households.

2025

Members debate the distinction between insured household lives and voting cooperative members.

2027

Kompas reports the broadest hospital network in this scenario, concentrated in its three core provinces.
