# Simulated Institutions and External Records

> **All institutions and documents below are fictional.** Their names and visual styles do not impersonate the Dutch Chamber of Commerce, Tax Administration, De Nederlandsche Bank, a real bank or a real newspaper.

## Included source families

- **Kamerregister voor Ondernemingen (KRO):** simulated company register extracts and relationship records.
- **Dienst voor Belastingen en Registratie (DBR):** simulated filing acknowledgements, which prove receipt only.
- **Nederlands Zorgtoezichtbureau (NZT):** simulated sector-level note on outsourced service concentration.
- **Noorder Cooperative Bank:** simulated bank facility summary for a fictional investment group.
- **De Zorglijn:** simulated specialist newsroom with two investigative pieces.

Each PDF is labelled SIMULATION ONLY. The documents are intentionally limited: a register can establish a filed identity, but not quality; a filing receipt is not an audit; a regulator-style note is not a finding against a named insurer; a newsroom article is a lead, not proof.
