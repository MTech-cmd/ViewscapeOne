## 4. Ownership, control and group structure

The annual report describes the group as a privately held company. That label needs to be separated from the voting and procurement rules. The shareholder record and management disclosures do not establish public ownership or member voting rights. [SRC01, SRC06]

**Research finding:** legal ownership, governance participation, operational control and service-provider relationships are four separate questions. Do not treat the corporate group chart as complete until filed ownership, board appointments and contracts are checked.

## 9. Public reputation and complaints

Published complaint/service figures are not comparable across the three insurers. The 78% figure counts hospital addresses with an active status in the 2027 directory export. It does not measure appointment capacity, specialties, treatment-level contracts or referral acceptance. The complaints rate counts tagged support tickets per active digital account, not only formal complaints. [SRC04, SRC08, SRC09]

**Finding:** the headline rates should be reported with their definitions, populations and exclusions. A ranking based on `3.8`, `5.6` and `3.1` alone would be unsound.
