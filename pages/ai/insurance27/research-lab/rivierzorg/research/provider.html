Care access review

Care areaMental healthcare

Digital intake and online therapy are readily available; face-to-face contracted options are thinner in rural areas. Wait estimates are supplied by provider, not independently measured.

Care areaGender-affirming healthcare

No dedicated in-house pathway. Referrals are made to external contracted providers; non-contracted specialist care may leave substantial out-of-pocket costs.

Care areaWomen's healthcare

Standard maternity and medically necessary gynaecological care through the preferred network; fewer independent clinics are listed in northern and rural areas.

Care areaDisability and rehabilitation

Medical aids are handled through selected suppliers; claims are digital-first and named case management is limited.

Care areaPhysiotherapy and dental

Start includes 6 sessions and 75% dental up to €250; major dental treatments may have a 12-month waiting period.

Practical-access assessment

  • Legal entitlement is not the same as contractual reimbursement.
  • Contractual reimbursement is not the same as a confirmed provider appointment.
  • A care pathway does not prove clinical waiting time.
