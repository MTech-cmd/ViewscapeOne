# Rivierzorg Direct - Brand and Experience Guide

> All marks, content and names are original fictional training assets.

## Brand concept

Liquid glass / modern console navigation / turquoise service telemetry

## Verbal identity

- Tagline: **Care, without the friction.**
- Secondary line: **Find the signal in the current.**
- Personality: Quietly confident, minimal, responsive, fluid, technical, clear and calm.
- Never imply that the visual design proves the product is better.

## Visual system

- Primary: `#00d6c5`
- Secondary: `#55f2dc`
- Accent: `#087fa1`
- Surface: `#0c2943`
- Ink: `#071c2b`

## Website expression

Translucent glass, turquoise/blue gradient fields, a horizontal XMB-style menu rail, soft motion, quiet metrics and an optional dark theme stored in the browser.

## Accessibility and motion

All navigation remains keyboard-focusable. Motion is decorative only; Rivierzorg respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Core information is in HTML, not embedded in images. The fictional status notice remains visible.

## Research presentation

Each company uses its brand vocabulary but the research layer retains common evidence semantics: source IDs, claim IDs, status, confidence, dates, limitations and unknowns.
