# Rivierzorg Direct - Fictional Company Wiki

> **FICTIONAL SIMULATION.** This is a secondary training article. The organisation, people, policies, prices, documents, events and figures are invented. It is not a real company, insurer, policy or legal record.

## Overview

A private digital-first group that treats health insurance like a connected service layer. It is fast at routine digital transactions; the difficult questions begin where the preferred network, external care and automated exceptions meet.

## Corporate profile

- **Parent group:** Riviergroep N.V.
- **Legal insurer:** Rivierzorg Verzekeringen N.V.
- **Public brand:** Rivierzorg Direct
- **Registered office (scenario):** Utrecht
- **Founded (scenario):** 2018
- **Insured population:** 325,000
- **Scenario market share:** 4.1% (scenario estimate)
- **Company model:** Privately held digital-first group

## History

- **2018:** Riviergroep is founded by a technology-led investment consortium to build a lower-cost digital insurer.
- **2019:** Rivierzorg launches a budget naturapolis supported by an app-first claims engine.
- **2020:** A preferred-provider network replaces the broad open network used in the pilot.
- **2022:** The group rolls out FlowCore, an automated authorisation and claims-routing platform.
- **2025:** The platform is connected to a third-party case and document relay for exceptions and manual review.
- **2026:** A service interruption delays a subset of document-dependent reimbursements; the group reports recovery within two working days.
- **2027:** The basic premium remains the lowest in this scenario, alongside the lowest non-contracted reimbursement rate.

## Insurance products

### Rivier Basis Select

**Basic insurance - €145.50 / month**
- Budget naturapolis; annual basic premium €1,746.00.
- Non-contracted care: 60% of the average contracted tariff subject to service-specific maximum amounts.
- Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
- The 78% network headline measures hospital addresses marked active in a directory snapshot, not contracted specialties or available appointments.
### Rivier Start

**Supplementary - €15.95 / month**
- 6 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €250 per policy year.
- Some complex dental treatments have a 12-month waiting period.
- App-based claims are supported; paper submission remains available for exceptions.
### Rivier Stroom

**Supplementary - €27.50 / month**
- 9 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €400 per policy year.
- Selected optical contribution up to €100 every two calendar years.
- Treatment at non-preferred supplementary providers may receive no benefit unless pre-authorised.

## Care access

- **Mental healthcare:** Digital intake and online therapy are readily available; face-to-face contracted options are thinner in rural areas. Wait estimates are supplied by provider, not independently measured.
- **Gender-affirming healthcare:** No dedicated in-house pathway. Referrals are made to external contracted providers; non-contracted specialist care may leave substantial out-of-pocket costs.
- **Women's healthcare:** Standard maternity and medically necessary gynaecological care through the preferred network; fewer independent clinics are listed in northern and rural areas.
- **Disability and rehabilitation:** Medical aids are handled through selected suppliers; claims are digital-first and named case management is limited.
- **Physiotherapy and dental:** Start includes 6 sessions and 75% dental up to €250; major dental treatments may have a 12-month waiting period.

## Customer service and public metrics

- **Headline premium:** €145.50 monthly.
- **Hospital network headline:** 78%.
- **Published complaints/service rate:** 5.6 / 1,000.

These measures are not directly comparable across the three fictional companies. Their numerators, denominators, periods and exclusions differ. The network figures describe administrative directory measures, not appointment access or clinical capacity.

## Governance, service partners and unresolved questions

The platform architecture describes an external exception route and a separate document relay. The service metrics exclude cases that require manual documents or review.

The source pack contains references to a shared administrative service environment. A service relationship is not proof of ownership, unlawful processing, shared claim decisions or common corporate control. The full contract, processing annex, subcontractor list and operational audit are not available.
