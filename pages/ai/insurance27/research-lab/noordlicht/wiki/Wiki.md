# Noordlicht Zorg - Fictional Company Wiki

> **FICTIONAL SIMULATION.** This is a secondary training article. The organisation, people, policies, prices, documents, events and figures are invented. It is not a real company, insurer, policy or legal record.

## Overview

A member-led insurer with a glossy future-facing public image and a dense regional history beneath it. Noordlicht grew from mutual-aid funds into a national group, but its most dependable relationships are still regional.

## Corporate profile

- **Parent group:** Noordlicht Holding U.A.
- **Legal insurer:** Noordlicht Zorgverzekeraar N.V.
- **Public brand:** Noordlicht Zorg
- **Registered office (scenario):** Zwolle
- **Founded (scenario):** 2001
- **Insured population:** 510,000
- **Scenario market share:** 6.4% (scenario estimate)
- **Company model:** Mutual-style cooperative group

## History

- **2001:** Three Overijssel and Gelderland mutual-aid funds combine under the Noordlicht name.
- **2007:** The group launches its first national basic policy while retaining a member council.
- **2014:** Noordlicht buys a small digital claims platform and brings first-line administration in-house.
- **2021:** The group starts the Halo pathway for complex and gender-affirming care in three university-hospital regions.
- **2025:** A multi-year case-administration agreement is extended; the public annual report describes it as a continuity partnership.
- **2027:** The company markets a national network while its detailed directory still shows regional differences by treatment and site.

## Insurance products

### Noordlicht Basis

**Basic insurance - €159.75 / month**
- Naturapolis; annual basic premium €1,917.00.
- Non-contracted care: 70% of the average contracted tariff as defined in the reimbursement schedule; caps and exclusions apply.
- Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
- Hospital directory: 92% headline figure; the source counts hospital organisations, not all treatment locations.
### Noordlicht Extra

**Supplementary - €20.90 / month**
- 9 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
- One optical contribution up to €75 every two calendar years.
- No automatic reimbursement for non-contracted supplementary providers; reimbursement depends on the supplementary schedule.
### Noordlicht Extra Plus

**Supplementary - €34.50 / month**
- 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €500 per policy year, subject to treatment-plan review.
- Optical contribution up to €125 every two calendar years.
- Some planned dental treatment requires prior acceptance; existing treatment plans may be excluded.

## Care access

- **Mental healthcare:** Broad national contracted network; triage target is 14 days to first contact. Treatment waits vary by provider and region.
- **Gender-affirming healthcare:** Halo pathway links three university-hospital regions; prior coordination is required for selected treatments. No source establishes one national appointment queue.
- **Women's healthcare:** Contracted midwifery and gynaecology; pelvic-floor physiotherapy depends on indication and provider contract. Endometriosis and menopause pathways vary by region.
- **Disability and rehabilitation:** Medical aids through contracted suppliers; a coordinator may be assigned after referral for eligible complex cases.
- **Physiotherapy and dental:** Extra includes 9 sessions and 75% dental up to €350; the basic-policy and supplementary entitlements are separate.

## Customer service and public metrics

- **Headline premium:** €159.75 monthly.
- **Hospital network headline:** 92%.
- **Published complaints/service rate:** 3.8 / 1,000.

These measures are not directly comparable across the three fictional companies. Their numerators, denominators, periods and exclusions differ. The network figures describe administrative directory measures, not appointment access or clinical capacity.

## Governance, service partners and unresolved questions

The company describes itself as member-owned. Its assembly minutes distinguish voting delegates from all insured people and state that supplier procurement remains with the executive board.

The source pack contains references to a shared administrative service environment. A service relationship is not proof of ownership, unlawful processing, shared claim decisions or common corporate control. The full contract, processing annex, subcontractor list and operational audit are not available.
