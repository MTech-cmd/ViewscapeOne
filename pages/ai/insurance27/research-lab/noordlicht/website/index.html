Basic insurance

Noordlicht Basis

€159.75 / month
  • Naturapolis; annual basic premium €1,917.00.
  • Non-contracted care: 70% of the average contracted tariff as defined in the reimbursement schedule; caps and exclusions apply.
  • Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
  • Hospital directory: 92% headline figure; the source counts hospital organisations, not all treatment locations.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002 and NOORDLICHT-SRC-007

Supplementary

Noordlicht Extra

€20.90 / month
  • 9 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
  • One optical contribution up to €75 every two calendar years.
  • No automatic reimbursement for non-contracted supplementary providers; reimbursement depends on the supplementary schedule.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002

Supplementary

Noordlicht Extra Plus

€34.50 / month
  • 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €500 per policy year, subject to treatment-plan review.
  • Optical contribution up to €125 every two calendar years.
  • Some planned dental treatment requires prior acceptance; existing treatment plans may be excluded.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002

✧ The Halo and care routes.

Mental healthcare

Broad national contracted network; triage target is 14 days to first contact. Treatment waits vary by provider and region.

Gender-affirming healthcare

Halo pathway links three university-hospital regions; prior coordination is required for selected treatments. No source establishes one national appointment queue.

Women's healthcare

Contracted midwifery and gynaecology; pelvic-floor physiotherapy depends on indication and provider contract. Endometriosis and menopause pathways vary by region.

Disability and rehabilitation

Medical aids through contracted suppliers; a coordinator may be assigned after referral for eligible complex cases.

Physiotherapy and dental

Extra includes 9 sessions and 75% dental up to €350; the basic-policy and supplementary entitlements are separate.

✦ A little company history.

2001 · Three Overijssel and Gelderland mutual-aid funds combine under the Noordlicht name.
2007 · The group launches its first national basic policy while retaining a member council.
2014 · Noordlicht buys a small digital claims platform and brings first-line administration in-house.
2021 · The group starts the Halo pathway for complex and gender-affirming care in three university-hospital regions.
2025 · A multi-year case-administration agreement is extended; the public annual report describes it as a continuity partnership.
2027 · The company markets a national network while its detailed directory still shows regional differences by treatment and site.

☻ Member governance - read the fine print.

The company calls itself member-owned. The member assembly record and procurement schedule are more specific about who votes, who advises and who can approve supplier agreements.
