Care access review

Care areaMental healthcare

Broad national contracted network; triage target is 14 days to first contact. Treatment waits vary by provider and region.

Care areaGender-affirming healthcare

Halo pathway links three university-hospital regions; prior coordination is required for selected treatments. No source establishes one national appointment queue.

Care areaWomen's healthcare

Contracted midwifery and gynaecology; pelvic-floor physiotherapy depends on indication and provider contract. Endometriosis and menopause pathways vary by region.

Care areaDisability and rehabilitation

Medical aids through contracted suppliers; a coordinator may be assigned after referral for eligible complex cases.

Care areaPhysiotherapy and dental

Extra includes 9 sessions and 75% dental up to €350; the basic-policy and supplementary entitlements are separate.

Provider choice and directory quality

92% is the headline directory figure. The 92% figure is based on hospital organisations in the public directory, not the number of sites or every specialty. The complaints rate excludes contacts resolved before a formal complaint is registered.

The directory is not a substitute for provider-level checks. Establish provider legal entity, site, specialty, treatment, contract period, referral, prior approval, capacity and waiting-time evidence.

Customer service and complaints

Published measure: 3.8 / 1,000.

The 92% figure is based on hospital organisations in the public directory, not the number of sites or every specialty. The complaints rate excludes contacts resolved before a formal complaint is registered.

Cross-company comparability: Disputed / High confidence. The three companies use materially different numerators and denominators.

Practical-access assessment

  • Legal entitlement is not the same as contractual reimbursement.
  • Contractual reimbursement is not the same as a confirmed provider appointment.
  • A care pathway does not prove clinical waiting time.
  • Supplementary benefits do not change the basic-policy provider network unless explicitly stated.
