# Noordlicht Zorg - Corporate and Product History

> Fictional timeline reconstructed from the scenario source pack.

| Year | Recorded event |
|---|---|
| 2001 | Three Overijssel and Gelderland mutual-aid funds combine under the Noordlicht name. |
| 2007 | The group launches its first national basic policy while retaining a member council. |
| 2014 | Noordlicht buys a small digital claims platform and brings first-line administration in-house. |
| 2021 | The group starts the Halo pathway for complex and gender-affirming care in three university-hospital regions. |
| 2025 | A multi-year case-administration agreement is extended; the public annual report describes it as a continuity partnership. |
| 2027 | The company markets a national network while its detailed directory still shows regional differences by treatment and site. |

## Identity changes

The simulation distinguishes the founding mutual/group, the contemporary parent group, the operating insurer and the public brand. These layers should never be collapsed into a single `company_name` field.

## Research questions

- Which events are supported by a primary record and which are narrative claims?
- Did a change in ownership, legal form or operating entity occur?
- Which subsidiaries provide insurance, administration, navigation, technology or procurement?
- Are current policy terms consistent with the historical description of the business?
