Policy dossier / 2027

The policy-year comparison is a separate layer from the corporate identity and provider-network assessment.

Fictional source environment: Noordlicht Zorg, all legal identities, people, documents, premiums, provider networks and metrics are invented. No actual coverage or legal position is described.

Product identity and scope

Policy year 2027 · Legal insurer: Noordlicht Zorgverzekeraar N.V. · Parent group: Noordlicht Holding U.A.

The assumed €385 deductible is a fictional scenario parameter. It is not a statement of the real statutory deductible. The basic policy and supplementary products must be analysed as separate contracts.

Basic insurance

Noordlicht Basis

€159.75 / month

  • Naturapolis; annual basic premium €1,917.00.
  • Non-contracted care: 70% of the average contracted tariff as defined in the reimbursement schedule; caps and exclusions apply.
  • Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
  • Hospital directory: 92% headline figure; the source counts hospital organisations, not all treatment locations.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002 and NOORDLICHT-SRC-007

Supplementary

Noordlicht Extra

€20.90 / month

  • 9 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
  • One optical contribution up to €75 every two calendar years.
  • No automatic reimbursement for non-contracted supplementary providers; reimbursement depends on the supplementary schedule.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002

Supplementary

Noordlicht Extra Plus

€34.50 / month

  • 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
  • Adult dental: 75% up to €500 per policy year, subject to treatment-plan review.
  • Optical contribution up to €125 every two calendar years.
  • Some planned dental treatment requires prior acceptance; existing treatment plans may be excluded.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002

Policy interpretation checks

  1. Confirm the legal insurer and policy version.
  2. Read the reimbursement tariff definition and maximum amounts.
  3. Check prior authorisation, referral and clinical indication rules.
  4. Separate per-year limits from per-treatment limits.
  5. Check waiting periods and planned-treatment exclusions.
  6. Check provider identity, location and policy-year contract status.
