## Product catalogue

### Noordlicht Basis

**Basic insurance | €159.75 / month**

- Naturapolis; annual basic premium €1,917.00.
- Non-contracted care: 70% of the average contracted tariff as defined in the reimbursement schedule; caps and exclusions apply.
- Voluntary deductible options in this exercise: up to €885 on top of the assumed €385 scenario deductible.
- Hospital directory: 92% headline figure; the source counts hospital organisations, not all treatment locations.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002 and NOORDLICHT-SRC-007.

### Noordlicht Extra

**Supplementary | €20.90 / month**

- 9 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €350 per policy year.
- One optical contribution up to €75 every two calendar years.
- No automatic reimbursement for non-contracted supplementary providers; reimbursement depends on the supplementary schedule.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002.

### Noordlicht Extra Plus

**Supplementary | €34.50 / month**

- 12 physiotherapy sessions per policy year.
- Adult dental: 75% up to €500 per policy year, subject to treatment-plan review.
- Optical contribution up to €125 every two calendar years.
- Some planned dental treatment requires prior acceptance; existing treatment plans may be excluded.

Source: NOORDLICHT-SRC-002.


## Calculation notes

- Monthly premium multiplied by twelve gives the annual basic premium only; add-on costs are separate.
- The assumed €385 deductible is a scenario parameter and should not be carried over into real-world advice.
- Non-contracted reimbursement percentages use a fictional insurer-defined tariff basis. They do not mean the same percentage of an invoice is paid.
- Annual caps are per policy year unless the wording says otherwise.
- Supplementary benefits do not alter the basic-policy contract or create unlimited provider choice.

## Exclusions and conditions to investigate

1. Provider contract and legal entity at the exact treatment location.
2. Whether an annual maximum is per insured person, household, treatment type or policy.
3. Prior authorisation and referral conditions.
4. Waiting periods and exclusions for planned or pre-existing treatment.
5. Treatment definitions, clinical criteria and approved codes.
6. Non-contracted care caps, maximum tariffs and administrative fees.
7. Whether unused sessions roll over and whether multiple packages can be combined.
