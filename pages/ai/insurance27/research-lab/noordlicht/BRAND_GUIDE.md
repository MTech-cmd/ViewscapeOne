# Noordlicht Zorg - Brand and Experience Guide

> All marks, content and names are original fictional training assets.

## Brand concept

Y2K cyber-goth / Windows 7 Aero / glittered desktop archive

## Verbal identity

- Tagline: **Zorg die ruimte geeft.**
- Secondary line: **A little light. A lot of layers.**
- Personality: Maximalist, playful, a little goth, optimistic, self-aware, bright and messy on purpose.
- Never imply that the visual design proves the product is better.

## Visual system

- Primary: `#ff2b9d`
- Secondary: `#ff76c8`
- Accent: `#b9a0ff`
- Surface: `#fce8f5`
- Ink: `#271224`

## Website expression

Floating Aero windows, taskbar, beveled buttons, browser chrome, neon-pink gradients, soft purple glass, faux desktop icons, playful marquee and intentionally busy information architecture. The main reading column keeps research content legible.

## Accessibility and motion

All navigation remains keyboard-focusable. Motion is decorative only; Rivierzorg respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Core information is in HTML, not embedded in images. The fictional status notice remains visible.

## Research presentation

Each company uses its brand vocabulary but the research layer retains common evidence semantics: source IDs, claim IDs, status, confidence, dates, limitations and unknowns.
